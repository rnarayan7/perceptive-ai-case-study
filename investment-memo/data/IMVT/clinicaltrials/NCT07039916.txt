Title: Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of IMVT-1402 in Participants With Mild to Severe Generalized Myasthenia Gravis
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE3
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Immunovant Sciences GmbH
Conditions: Generalized Myasthenia Gravis
Interventions: IMVT-1402 (DRUG), IMVT-1402 (DRUG), Placebo (DRUG), IMVT-1402 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 231

Summary: The purpose of the study is to assess the efficacy, safety and tolerability of IMVT-1402 in adult participants with mild to severe generalized myasthenia gravis.

Primary outcomes:
- Change from Baseline in MG-ADL Score for Antibody-positive Participants (time frame: Baseline to Week 12)
