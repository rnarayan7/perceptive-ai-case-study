Title: Study to Assess Batoclimab in Participants With Active Thyroid Eye Disease
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Immunovant Sciences GmbH
Conditions: Thyroid Eye Disease
Interventions: Batoclimab (DRUG), Placebo (DRUG)
Enrollment: 86

Summary: To evaluate the efficacy of batoclimab 680 milligrams (mg) subcutaneous (SC) once a week (QW) for 12 weeks followed by 340 mg SC QW for 12 weeks versus placebo on proptosis responder rate at Week 24.

Primary outcomes:
- Percentage of proptosis responders (time frame: At Week 24)
