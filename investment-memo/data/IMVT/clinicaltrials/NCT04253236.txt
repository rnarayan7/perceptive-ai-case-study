Title: To Assess the Efficacy and Safety of RVT-1401 in the Treatment of Warm Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia (ASCEND-WAIHA).
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: PHASE2
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Immunovant Sciences GmbH
Conditions: Warm Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia
Interventions: RVT-1401 680 mg/weekly (DRUG), RVT-1401 340 mg/weekly (DRUG)
Enrollment: 5

Summary: This is a Phase 2 non-randomized, open-label study to investigate the efficacy, safety and tolerability of RVT-1401 in patients with Warm Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia.

Primary outcomes:
- Number of Responders at Week 13 (time frame: Week 13)
- Number of Participants With Any Treatment-emergent Adverse Event (TEAE), Serious AE (SAE), Treatment-related Adverse Event (AE), and Death (time frame: Up to Week 20)
