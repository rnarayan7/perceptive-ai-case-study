Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On September 2, 2026, Immunovant, Inc. (“Immunovant”) held its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). There were 198,373,370 shares of common stock and Series A preferred stock represented at the Annual Meeting by valid proxies or voted at the Annual Meeting, which was approximately 96% of the shares of common stock and Series A preferred stock entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. At the Annual Meeting, Immunovant’s stockholders voted on the three proposals set forth below. A more detailed description of each proposal is set forth in Immunovant’s definitive proxy statement for the Annual Meeting (the “Proxy Statement”), which was filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on July 22, 2026.

Proposal 1 – Election of Directors

Jacob Bauer, Douglas Hughes and Robert Susman were each elected to serve as a member of Immunovant’s Board of Directors (the “Board”), until the 2027 Annual Meeting of Stockholders and until their successors are duly elected and qualified, by the following votes:
