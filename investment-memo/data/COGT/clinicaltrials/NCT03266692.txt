Title: Study of ACTR087 in Combination With SEA-BCMA in Subjects With Relapsed or Refractory Multiple Myeloma
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: PHASE1
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Cogent Biosciences, Inc.
Conditions: Multiple Myeloma, Multiple Myeloma in Relapse, Refractory Multiple Myeloma
Interventions: ACTR087 (BIOLOGICAL), SEA-BCMA (BIOLOGICAL)
Enrollment: 15

Summary: This is a phase 1, multi-center, single-arm, open-label study evaluating the safety, tolerability, and anti-myeloma activity of ACTR087 (an autologous T cell product) in combination with SEA-BCMA (a monoclonal antibody) in subjects with relapsed or refractory Multiple Myeloma.

Primary outcomes:
- Safety and tolerability of ACTR087 in combination with SEA-BCMA (time frame: 28 days)
- Determination of recommended Phase 2 dosing regimen (time frame: 52 weeks)
