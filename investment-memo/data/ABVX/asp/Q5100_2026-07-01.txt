Medicare Part B average sales price (ASP) for ustekinumab (Inj ustekinumab-kfce, 1 mg, HCPCS Q5100). ASP net price is $3.1038 per 1 MG billing unit, derived from the Part B payment limit of $3.2900 (ASP plus the 6% add-on), effective 2026 Q3 (2026-07-01 through 2026-09-30). ASP is the net-of-rebate average sales price and serves as the branded net-price benchmark for this physician-administered drug. This payment limit is based on 1Q26 ASP data.
