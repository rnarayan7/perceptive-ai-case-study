Title: ABX196 in Combination With Nivolumab in Patients With Hepatocellular Carcinoma
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE1, PHASE2
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Abivax S.A.
Conditions: Carcinoma, Hepatocellular
Interventions: ABX196 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 48

Summary: Open-label, uncontrolled, phase 1-2 study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, pharmacodynamic effects, and preliminary efficacy of ABX196 administered in combination with nivolumab in patients with hepatocellular carcinoma

Primary outcomes:
- Incidence of adverse events (AEs) (time frame: Through study completion, an average of 1 year)
