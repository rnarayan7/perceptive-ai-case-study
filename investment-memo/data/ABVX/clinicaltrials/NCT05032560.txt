Title: Safety and Pharmacokinetics Study in Healthy Japanese Volunteers
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Abivax S.A.
Conditions: Healthy Volunteers
Interventions: ABX464 (DRUG), Placebo (DRUG)
Enrollment: 48

Summary: The study consists of Part A, a randomized double-blind, single-ascending-dose study, and Part B, a randomized, double-blind, semi-sequential, escalating multiple-dose study, in healthy Japanese volunteers.

Primary outcomes:
- Incidence of adverse events (AEs) and SAEs (time frame: 6 weeks)
- incidence of treatment-emergent serious adverse events (time frame: 6 weeks)
- incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events of special interest (AESIs). (time frame: 6 weeks)
- incidence of clinically significant laboratory abnormalities (time frame: 6 weeks)
