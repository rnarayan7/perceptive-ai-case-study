Title: ABX464 First in Man Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Abivax S.A.
Conditions: Healthy Volunteers
Interventions: ABX464 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 24

Summary: This study is an open label, parallel group, single ascending dose, exploratory study performed in a single site (Centre Cap, Montpellier, France).

Primary outcomes:
- Percentage of patients experiencing at least one Adverse Event (time frame: Up to 45 days post dosing)
