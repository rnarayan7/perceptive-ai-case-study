Title: A Clinical Trial to Evaluate the Relative Bioavailability of PRAX-628
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Praxis Precision Medicines
Conditions: Healthy
Interventions: PRAX-628 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 16

Summary: A phase I clinical trial to evaluate the relative bioavailability of PRAX-628 tablet formulation compared to capsule formulation

Primary outcomes:
- To assess the relative bioavailability of single 40 mg oral doses of PRAX-628 tablet as compared to PRAX-628 capsules (time frame: 28 days)
- To assess the relative bioavailability of single 40 mg oral doses of PRAX-628 tablet as compared to PRAX-628 capsules (time frame: 28 days)
