Title: A Clinical Trial of 2 Doses of PRAX-944 in Participants With Essential Tremor
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Praxis Precision Medicines
Conditions: Essential Tremor
Interventions: 100 mg PRAX-944 (DRUG), 60 mg PRAX-944 (DRUG), Placebo (DRUG), Flexibly dosed 20 mg to 100 mg PRAX-944 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 133

Summary: This multi-center, randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled, dose-range-finding clinical trial (with an optional Extension comprised of an Extension Double-blind (DB) Lead in Period followed by an Extension Open-label (OL) Period) that will assess the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of PRAX 944 in participants aged 18 years or older who have a diagnosis of Essential Tremor (ET) and have had symptoms for at least 3 years.

Primary outcomes:
- Change from baseline to Day 56 on the modified ADL (time frame: 56 days)
