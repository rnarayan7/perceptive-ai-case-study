Title: An Open Label Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of PRAX-628 in Adults With Focal Onset or Tonic-Clonic Seizures
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE2
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Praxis Precision Medicines
Conditions: Focal Onset Seizure, Primary Generalized Epilepsy
Interventions: 30mg PRAX-628 (DRUG)
Enrollment: 50

Summary: An Open Label Clinical Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of PRAX-628 in Adult Patients With Focal Onset or Primary Generalized Tonic-Clonic Seizures

Primary outcomes:
- To evaluate the efficacy of PRAX-628 on seizure frequency in adults with FOS or PGTCS currently taking ASMs (time frame: 8 weeks)
