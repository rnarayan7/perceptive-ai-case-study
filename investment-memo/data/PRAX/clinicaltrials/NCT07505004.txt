Title: Randomized Clinical Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Vormatrigine in Adults With Focal Seizures
Status: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE3
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Praxis Precision Medicines
Conditions: Focal Epilepsy
Interventions: 40 mg/day vormatrogine for 12 weeks (DRUG), 30 mg/day vormatrogine for 12 weeks (DRUG), 20 mg/day vormatrogine for 12 weeks (DRUG), Placebo (DRUG)
Enrollment: 300

Summary: A multicenter, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial to evaluate the efficacy and safety of vormatrigine in adults with focal seizures (POWER2)

Primary outcomes:
- To evaluate the efficacy of vormatrigine compared to placebo on focal seizure frequency in adults currently taking 1 to 3 ASMs (time frame: 12 weeks)
