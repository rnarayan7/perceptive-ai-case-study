Title: A Clinical Trial of Elsunersen in Pediatric SCN2A-DEE to Assess Efficacy and Safety
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE3
Study type: INTERVENTIONAL
Lead sponsor: Praxis Precision Medicines
Conditions: Epileptic Encephalopathy, SCN2A Encephalopathy
Interventions: 1mg elsunersen (DRUG), 0.5mg elsunersen (DRUG)
Enrollment: 40

Summary: A Multi-Center, Single-Arm Clinical Trial to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Elsunersen in Pediatric Participants with Early Onset SCN2A Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy

Primary outcomes:
- To assess the efficacy of elsunersen on seizure frequency in participants with early-onset SCN2A DEE (time frame: 24 weeks)
