Title: Evaluation of Cutaneous and Circulating Inflammatory Biomarkers in Hidradenitis Suppurativa and Atopic Dermatitis
Status: COMPLETED
Study type: OBSERVATIONAL
Lead sponsor: Kymera Therapeutics, Inc.
Conditions: Hidradenitis Suppurativa, Dermatitis, Atopic
Enrollment: 40

Summary: This non-intervention study will identify the biomarker profiles in hidradenitis suppurativa (HS) and atopic dermatitis (AD) (as a comparator) that would have the most utility in interventional studies evaluating efficacy during therapeutic intervention. Study data will also be used to correlate cellular/molecular changes in blood (cells and serum), and tissue, with clinical/histopathological phenotypes.

Primary outcomes:
- Biomarker Identification (time frame: Day 1)
- Determination of Biomarker Correlations (time frame: Day 1)
- Examination of IRAK4 Degradation Effects Ex Vivo (time frame: Day 1)
