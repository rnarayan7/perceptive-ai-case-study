Fluorescence plasma spot analyses (FPSA) in samples taken from mice injected with antibodies (Abs) and antibody drug conjugates (ADCs) by different routes. Mean (+SD) FPSA values from each group were plotted over time (A). Pharmacokinetic profile of ADC-fluorophore (F) clearance from each group of mice as determined using ELISA over 168 h (B). Linear regression of ADC-F FPSA vs. concentration as determined using each method (C). ADC3 is Ab3 conjugated with camptothecin, both target human Sialyl-Thomsen-nouveau (non-mouse cross reactive). IV: Intravenous administration; IP: intraperitoneal administration. The standardized FPSA maximum fluorescence intensities were higher for the IV versus IP dosed groups and the cumulative fluorescence intensities were higher for the Ab3-F versus ADC3-F dosed groups ( Figure 3 A). Quantitative analysis of matching plasma samples using ELISA confirmed that the maximum observed concentrations (C max ) for the IV dosed groups were higher than the IP dosed groups and the area under curve (AUC) was higher for Ab3-F versus ADC3-F dosed groups (Figure 3B). The standardized FPSA and ELISA results were strongly correlated across groups (Figure 3C; r=0.96, p <0.0001).
