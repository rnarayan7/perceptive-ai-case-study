Pooled analysis—results of OS A pooled analysis of seven studies reporting OS demonstrated that the intervention group achieved a significant improvement in OS compared with the control group (HR = 0.79, 95%CI: 0.71-0.87, p<0.001), with negligible heterogeneity (I²=0%), as shown in Figure 4 . Sensitivity analysis was performed by sequentially excluding each study, and the pooled HR and 95%CI remained stable without substantial changes, supporting the robustness of the PFS result, as shown in Supplementary Figure 4 .
