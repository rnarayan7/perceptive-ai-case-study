FcRn, neonatal Fc receptor; fr, fraction recycled to the plasma compartment; CL TP,L , extravasation clearance via large pores; CL TP,S , extravasation clearance via small pores. Briefly, the PBPK model characterizes PK of biologics of varying size including monoclonal antibodies, Fc‐fusion proteins, albumin, albumin conjugates, and albumin‐binding proteins using a whole‐body structure (Figure 1A ). Protein catabolism was modeled by uptake and degradation in lysosomes within endothelial cells and recycling via the neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) (Figure 1B ).
