Forest plot analysis of overall survival in all GC/GEJ patients. Pooled hazard ratios for overall survival comparing PD- (L) 1 inhibitor plus chemotherapy versus chemotherapy alone in HER2-negative advanced GC/GEJ. Chemo-immunotherapy was associated with a significant improvement in OS (pooled HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.75–0.84; p < 0.0001) with no detectable between-study heterogeneity demonstrating a consistent treatment effect across studies ( Figure 2 ).
