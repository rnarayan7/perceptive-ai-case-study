Spider plot showing the percent change in maximum tumor diameter from baseline in patients with lung metastases treated with lenvatinib ( n  = 25) Each line represents the best percentage change in the largest measurable tumor for an individual patient with lung metastasis. Baseline tumor burden: median maximum tumor diameter, 45.0 mm (range, 27.0–81.7); median diameter of the single largest lesion, 25.6 mm (15.2–46.3). Best overall response: CR 0, PR 16, SD 9; ORR 64.0%; DCR 100%. Median maximum tumor change: –33.5% (range, –91.9 to –2.3). Median time to first response: 2.5 months. The red triangles indicate the time points when treatment was switched from lenvatinib to BRAF/MEK inhibitors, and the blue triangles indicate when treatment was switched from BRAF/MEK inhibitors to lenvatinib. Abbreviations : CR, complete response; PR, partial response; SD, stable disease; ORR, objective response rate; DCR, disease control rate; mos., months; LEN, lenvatinib; BRAF/MEK, BRAF/MEK inhibitor.
