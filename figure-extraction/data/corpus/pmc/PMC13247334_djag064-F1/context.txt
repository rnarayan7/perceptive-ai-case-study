Objective response rate  (A)  and time to surgery  (B)  for LS-004 single-arm trial and natural history study external control arm participants (propensity score weighting). Abbreviations: NH Study = Natural History Study; LS-004 = LITESPARK [LS]-004 ( NCT03401788 ). Date of Data Cut-off: 01APR2022 for LS-004, 05AUG2022 for NH Study. Panel A excludes 11 of the 178 patients from the ORR analysis who had renal surgery and less than 3 serial image assessments (baseline and post patient level index date) required for the RECIST assessment within 5 years of follow-up. Panel B includes an effective sample size of 60 patients in the NH Study after PS weighting. The PS weighted estimate (95% CI) for ORR was 63.9% (95% CI = 51.9 to 76.0) in LS-004 and 1.5% (95% CI = 0.0 to 3.3) for the NH Study ECA ( Figure 1 , Panel A ). The median TTS for the NH Study ECA was 51.3 (95% CI = 43.8 to not yet reached) months and not yet reached in LS-004 ( Figure 1 , Panel B ).
