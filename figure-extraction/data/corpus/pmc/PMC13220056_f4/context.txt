Forest plots of tumor response outcomes.  (A)  Random-effects model forest plot of objective response rate (ORR).  (B)  Fixed-effects model forest plot of complete response (CR) rate. All effect estimates lie in the OR > 1 region, indicating superior ORR and CR with ICI-TKI compared with sunitinib. Objective response rate and complete response Across five RCTs (n = 3,637), ICI-TKI showed a significant advantage in ORR over sunitinib (pooled odds ratio [OR] 3.74, 95% CI 2.63–5.32, P < 0.0001) ( Figure 4A ). Among the same patient population (n = 3,637), the CR rate significantly favored ICI-TKI therapy over sunitinib (OR 3.16, 95% CI 2.36–4.22, P < 0.0001) ( Figure 4B ). Excluding CLEAR and CheckMate 9ER reduced I² to 0% and RR remained 1.10, suggesting that potent vascular endothelial growth factor receptor-tyrosine kinase inhibitor (VEGFR-TKI) plus PD-1 blockade may be a key contributor to this variability ( Supplementary Figure 4 ).
