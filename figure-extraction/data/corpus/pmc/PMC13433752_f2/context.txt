Forest plot of the association between tumoral Siglec-15 expression and OS in solid tumors. HR, hazard ratio; OS, overall survival. Pooled analysis showed that high Siglec-15 expression was significantly associated with worse OS in solid tumors (HR = 1.53, 95% CI: 1.25-1.86, P < 0.001, Figure 2 ). Subgroup analyses confirmed the robustness of this association across multiple stratifications, including country, sample size, HR type, data source, scoring method, cutoff definition, and study quality ( Table 2 ; Supplementary Figures 1 – 7 ).
