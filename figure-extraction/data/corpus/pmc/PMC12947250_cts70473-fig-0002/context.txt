Mean plasma concentration‐time profiles of QX008N following single‐dose administration. (A) Linear‐scale profile. (B) Semi‐logarithmic‐scale profile. For both panels, the main graph depicts the complete pharmacokinetic profile from 0 to 127 days (3048 h). The inset in each panel provides a detailed view of the absorption and early distribution phases from 0 to 72 h. The dashed horizontal line in each panel represents the lower limit of quantification (LLOQ) of the bioanalytical assay (0.08 μg/mL). Data are presented as mean ± SD (error bars may be smaller than the symbol size at later time points). SC, subcutaneous; IV, intravenous. Pharmacokinetics After a single subcutaneous injection of 280 mg, 560 mg, and intravenous infusion of 560 mg QX008N, mean plasma concentration‐time profiles of QX008N following single‐dose administration are shown in Figure 2 .
