Hematological response to azacitidine therapy. ( A ) Change in median hemoglobin (g/L) and platelet count (×10 9 /L) from baseline to the best-achieved value. ( B ) Change in median absolute neutrophil count (×10 9 /L) from baseline to the best-achieved value. Abbreviations: ANC, Absolute neutrophil count; plt, platelet count; Hb, Hemoglobin; Tx, treatment. Hematologic responses were observed in both the younger and elderly subgroups as shown in Figure 4 below. In the elderly subgroup, median hemoglobin improved from 80 g/L (range: 56–140) at baseline to 108 g/L (range: 70–155) ( Figure 4 A). In the elderly subgroup, the median baseline ANC was 0.89 × 10 9 /L (range: 0.02–58 × 10 9 /L), which improved to 2.2 × 10 9 /L (range: 0.04–9.6 × 10 9 /L) with treatment ( Figure 4 B). In the elderly subgroup, median platelet counts rose from 75 × 10 9 /L (range: 16–558 × 10 9 /L) to 155 × 10 9 /L (range: 10–767 × 10 9 /L) on AZA ( Figure 4 A).
