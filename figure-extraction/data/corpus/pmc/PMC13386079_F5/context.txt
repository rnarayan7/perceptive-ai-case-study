Overall survival and ctDNA levels in OC patients treated at pharmacodynamically active doses of ≥90 µg. ( A ) Kaplan-Meier estimates of overall survival and ( B ) waterfall plot of best percent change in ctDNA levels at week 7 (C3D1) from baseline by MAGE-A4 expression (negative, n=6; unknown, n=3; or positive, n=14) for evaluable patients with OC treated at target doses ≥90 µg IMC-C103C. ctDNA, circulating tumor DNA; OC, ovarian carcinoma; unk, unknown. A trend to longer OS was observed in patients with MAGE-A4 + tumors with a 6-month OS rate of 89% (95% CI 61% to 97%) vs 58% (95% CI 23% to 82%) in patients without detectable MAGE-A4 expression ( figure 5A ). Of the 14 evaluable patients, eight (57%) had any reduction, including three who achieved a ctDNA molecular response (≥ 0.5 log (68%) reduction) ( figure 5B ).
