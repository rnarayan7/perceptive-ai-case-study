Progression-free survival Similarly, LCT was associated with a significant prolongation of progression-free survival (HR = 0.53, 95% CI 0.46-0.61, Figure 3A ). Cumulative meta-analysis showed that the PFS benefit emerged early and remained stable over successive studies, with the final pooled HR reaching 0.53 in 2025 ( Figure 3B ). Sensitivity analyses for the outcomes Leave-one-out sensitivity analyses confirmed the robustness of the pooled estimates ( Supplementary Figure 3 ).
