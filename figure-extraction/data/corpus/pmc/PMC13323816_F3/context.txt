The mean concentration-time profile of STSA-1002 in the 5 mg/kg, 10 mg/kg, and 20 mg/kg cohorts (semi-logarithmic) (n = 20). Healthy subjects received STSA-1002 at 5 mg/kg (n = 4), 10 mg/kg (n = 8), or 20 mg/kg (n = 8) once weekly for 4 weeks. Blood samples were collected pre-dose and at multiple time points up to Day 56. Plasma concentrations of STSA-1002 were measured using a validated ELISA method. Data are presented as mean ± standard deviation (SD), with error bars representing SD. The y-axis is shown on a logarithmic scale (base 10) to accommodate the wide concentration range. No statistical comparisons were performed for this figure; the plot is intended for descriptive illustration of the pharmacokinetic profiles. The y-axis is shown on a logarithmic scale (base 10).
