Kaplan–Meier progression-free survival curves according to systemic immune-inflammation index groups. Patients with high SII experienced significantly shorter progression-free survival than patients with low SII (median progression-free survival: 7.2 vs. 15.6 months; log-rank  p  = 0.018). Median progression-free survival was 15.6 months (95% CI, 8.2–23.1) in the low-SII group and 7.2 months (95% CI, 4.5–9.8) in the high-SII group (log-rank p = 0.018; Figure 2 ).
