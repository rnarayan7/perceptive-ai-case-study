Forest plot of hazard ratios for PFS (A)  and OS  (B)  in patients with PLELC receiving first-line chemo-immunotherapy versus chemotherapy alone. PLELC, pulmonary lymphoepithelioma like carcinoma; PFS, Progression-Free Survival;OS, overall survival. Overall survival (OS) also improved significantly, with a 52% reduction in mortality risk for the combination group (pooled HR = 0.48, 95% CI: 0.37–0.62, P < 0.001; I² = 0%) ( Figure 2 , Table 2 ).
