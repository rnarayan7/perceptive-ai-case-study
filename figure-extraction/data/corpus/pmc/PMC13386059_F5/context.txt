Serum cytokine changes during treatment and associations with clinical outcome. ( A ) Paired comparison of serum cytokine levels between pretreatment and week-5 samples, shown as log₂ fold change. Each point represents an individual patient. P values were computed using paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Cytokines with significant changes (p<0.05) are indicated with an asterisk. ( B–C ) Cox proportional hazards regression evaluating baseline cytokine concentrations and their association with ( B ) progression-free survival and ( C ) OS. HRs with 95% CIs are shown. HR >1 indicates increased risk, while HR <1 indicates reduced risk. Statistically significant associations (p<0.05) are annotated with an asterisk. n=11. PFS, progression-free survival; OS, overall survival. The results showed an increase in most cytokines during the first 4 weeks, with IL-8 exhibiting the most significant increase followed by interferon-gamma-induced protein 10 (IP-10) ( figure 5A ). In general, higher baseline cytokine levels tended to be associated with prolonged OS and PFS (OS: HR <1 for 18/19 cytokines; PFS: HR <1 for 16/19 cytokines) ( figure 5B,C ). RANTES and IL-4 were significantly associated with improved OS (unadjusted p values; figure 5C ), while basic fibroblast growth factor (FGF basic) was borderline significant (p=0.06). None of the cytokines showed a significant association with PFS ( figure 5B ), or with the incidence of irAEs ( online supplemental figure S8A ).
