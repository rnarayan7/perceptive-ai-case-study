Forest plot of overall survival in patients with hormone receptor–positive de novo metastatic breast cancer receiving local treatment of the primary tumor versus systemic therapy alone. Although a trend toward improved survival was observed, the effect did not reach statistical significance (HR=0.78, 95% CI=0.58–1.04). In hormone receptor–positive disease, local treatment showed a favorable – but non-significant – trend toward improved OS (HR=0.78, 95% CI=0.58–1.04, p =0.0885; Figure 4 ).
