Single-dose pharmacokinetics of laroprovstat. A , Geometric mean±SD plasma concentration after a single oral dose in the fasted state of laroprovstat 30 mg (n=6), 60 mg (n=6), 120 mg (n=6), 200 mg (n=5), and 400 mg (n=6).  B , Geometric mean±SD plasma concentration of laroprovstat 120 mg after a single oral dose in fasted state or 30 minutes after a meal (fed). gSD indicates geometric SD. Pharmacokinetics Participants were dosed orally in the fasted state with 30, 60, 120, 200, and 400 mg laroprovstat, with the concentration-time profiles presented in Figure 4 A. The geometric mean ratio for AUC inf and C max in the fed versus fasted stated were 1.15 (90% CI, 1.11–1.19) and 1.06 (90% CI, 1.00–1.13), respectively (Figure 4 B).
