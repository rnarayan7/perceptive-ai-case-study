TAK‐500 population PK‐PD model in cynomolgus monkey. (A) The model structure represents the population PK‐PD model, linking the PK profile of total antibody and conjugated payload of TAK‐500 with receptor occupancy and monocyte migration from the circulation. (B–M) The plots represent overlay of observed (obs) vs. model predicted (sim) PK profile of (B–D) total antibody, (E–G) conjugated payload in cynomolgus monkey when administered with TAK‐500, administered intravenously at 0.1, 0.5, 3 mg/kg, respectively. The plots (H–J) represent receptor occupancy, and (K–M) denote monocyte counts in the circulation.
