Local sensitivity study of liver protein AUC (area under the curve) ( A ) and liver mRNA AUC ( B ). Each of the parameters was varied by 50% (decreased or increased), and corresponding fold changes in mRNA/protein AUC from the baseline were plotted. Red bars indicate the fold change due to the increase in the parameter by 50%, and the blue bars indicate the fold change as a result of the parameter reduction by 50%. mRNA dose is fixed to 2 mg/kg and the infusion time is 10 min
