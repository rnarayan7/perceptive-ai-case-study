Differential gene expression analysis across multiple timepoints. a  Volcano plot showing differentially expressed genes at block 2 week 4 (B2W4, post-10 doses of IL-2) versus block 2 week 2 (B2W2, post-4 doses of pembrolizumab), with significant genes (FDR < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.6) highlighted in red. Statistical significance was determined by two-sided paired  t -test with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction ( n  = 15 paired samples from patients).  b ,  c  Volcano plots of extreme versus non-responders at B2W2 ( b ) and B2W4 ( c ), with significant genes ( p  < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.6) highlighted. Statistical significance was determined by two-sided Welch’s  t -test;  p -values are uncorrected as results did not survive FDR correction. At B2W2: EXT,  n  = 7; NON,  n  = 3 patients. At B2W4: EXT,  n  = 8; NON,  n  = 3 patients.  d  Heatmap of gene expression at B2W4 showing individual patient expression profiles (EXT,  n  = 8; INT,  n  = 6; NON,  n  = 3 patients) for selected inflammatory mediators and chemokines. Color intensity represents Z-scored expression levels; red indicates upregulation and blue indicates downregulation.
