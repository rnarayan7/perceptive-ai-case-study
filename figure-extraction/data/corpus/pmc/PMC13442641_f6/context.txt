Patients in the high-risk subgroups (L3–4, L5–6) presented remarkably shorter OS (P< 0.0001) and PFS (P = 0.00014) ( Figures 6A, B ). The POD24 incidence increased gradually from 6.0% (L0 group) to 29.0% (L5–6 group) (P = 0.011; Figure 6C ).
