Methylation tumor fraction clearance and association with outcome. A  Spider plot outlining time to mTF clearance over time ( n  = 18 patients with clearance). Median 7.3 months (range: 0.7–30.6 months) indicated with vertical dotted line.  B  Forest plot highlighting baseline clinical features and association with mTF clearance ( n  = 57). Point estimates represent odds ratios from univariable logistic regression models (generalized linear model with binomial family and logit link); horizontal lines represent 95% confidence intervals derived from Wald-type standard errors.  P -values are two-sided and derived from Wald tests. No adjustments were made for multiple comparisons. Association between mTF clearance and ( C ) time to CDK4/6i discontinuation or ( D ) overall survival ( n  = 54; three patients with undetectable mTF at baseline were excluded from the clearance analysis). Survival curves were estimated using the Kaplan–Meier method; shaded bands represent 95% confidence intervals. Median survival is indicated where crossed.  P -values and hazard ratios with 95% confidence intervals were derived from two-sided Cox proportional hazards models.  n  = 36 (no clearance),  n  = 18 (clearance).
