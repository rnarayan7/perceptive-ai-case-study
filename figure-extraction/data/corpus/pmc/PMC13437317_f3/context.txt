As illustrated in Supplementary Figure 3a , everolimus treatment for 3–6 months resulted in a marked reduction in tumor size. Notably, in 5 patients (7.46%), the RAML volume exceeded the baseline level at 3 months after discontinuation; this number increased to 28 patients (47.46%) at 6 months ( Supplementary Figure 3b ). 12.66 (9.20–17.41) cm³, P <.001], indicating a weaker response to everolimus in fat-rich lesions ( Table 3 and Figure 3a ). In contrast, all fat-poor RAML group patients exhibited a reduction in RAML tumour volume; 96.30% (26/27) demonstrated a ≥30% reduction in RAML tumour volume, and an identical proportion of the patients exhibited a ≥ 50% reduction ( Figure 3b ). After 6 months of treatment, the mean CT value decreased in both groups, with a more considerable reduction observed in the fat-poor RAML group ( Table 3 ; Figure 3c ). In contrast, the mean CT value decreased in all fat-poor RAML group patients ( Figure 3d ).
