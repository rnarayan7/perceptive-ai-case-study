Results of the a) ADCC, B) ADCP FcyRI, C) ADCP FcyRIIa and D) CDC cell-based reporter assays. Mean luminescence signals from three donors are presented with Procizumab (PCZ) tested either alone (green; 0.001–1 mg/mL) or with DPP3 (turquoise and orange; 10 and 100 ng/mL); results from the isotype control are shown in dark blue negative control (w/o analyte; NC) and positive control (anti-CD20 IgG1 antibody; PC) are displayed in a separate graph. ADCC – antibody-dependent cell mediated cytotoxicity; ADCP – antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis; CDC – complement-dependent cytotoxicity; FcR – Fc receptor. PCZ was not found to have effects related to ADCC, ADCP or CDC in human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) ( Figure 2 ) confirming the absence of unwanted activation of the immune system via these pathways.
