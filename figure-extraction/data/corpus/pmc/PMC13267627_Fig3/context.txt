Efficacy outcomes in the intention-to-treat population. ( A ) Kaplan-Meier estimates of progression-free survival (PFS) according to treatment cohort (Q3W vs. Q4W). ( B ) Waterfall plot showing the best percentage change in the sum of target lesion diameters from baseline for individual patients. Note: Patients with non-measurable disease at baseline (n = 6) were excluded from the waterfall plot analysis, which includes only the 20 patients with measurable target lesions. ( C ) Spider plot illustrating the longitudinal percentage change in target lesion size from baseline over time. Blue bars and lines indicate the Q3W cohort, and red bars and lines indicate the Q4W cohort. The horizontal dashed lines represent the thresholds for progressive disease (+ 20%) and partial response (− 30%) according to RECIST v1.1 criteria. Patients with non-measurable disease at baseline were excluded from the analysis of tumor size change (Panels  B  and  C ).
