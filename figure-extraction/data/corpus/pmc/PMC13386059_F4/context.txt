Association of immune cell subsets in PBMCs with irAEs. ( A ) Principal component analysis of major immune cell subset frequencies. Samples are colored according to irAE status (yes/no). PC1 and PC2 together capture major variance in the baseline immune landscape, showing partial separation between patients who did and did not develop irAEs. n=12. ( B–C ) Logistic regression analysis showing ORs for any-grade irAEs according to ( B ) major immune cell subsets and ( C ) functional T-cell phenotypes, measured in PBMCs at baseline and week 5. OR >1 indicates increased risk of developing irAEs, whereas OR <1 indicates reduced risk. Statistically significant associations (p<0.05) are indicated with an asterisk. 95% CIs are given. CIs extending beyond the plotted range (OR >100 or <0.01) are truncated for visualization. CM, central memory; EM, effector memory; irAE, immune-related adverse event; PBMCs, peripheral blood mononuclear cells; TEMRA, terminally differentiated effector memory T cells re-expressing CD45RA; Treg, regulatory T cell. A principal component analysis revealed a partial separation between patients with and without irAEs ( figure 4A ). Specifically, higher levels of regulatory T cells (Tregs), CD4 + EM, Th1 and ICOS + CD8 T cells at baseline were associated with increased risk of developing irAEs ( figure 4B,C ).
