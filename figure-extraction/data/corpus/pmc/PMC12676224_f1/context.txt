A comprehensive visualization of efficacy evaluation for all PP population patients is presented in Figure 1 . The spider plot ( Figure 1A ) clearly demonstrates the therapeutic response dynamics of each patient over time. The waterfall plot analysis of PP population ( Figure 1B ) revealed, three confirmed CR cases, one patient achieved 100% disappearance of target lesions but failed to maintain this response for ≥4 weeks (classified as PR), three SD cases showed >30% reduction in target lesions without sustaining for 4 weeks, nine patients met PD criteria with >20% increase in target lesions. PFS outcomes for the PP population are shown in Figure 1C .
