Efficacy of rogocekib in patients with solid tumors. Patients with measurable target tumor lesions were analyzed for maximum tumor shrinkage. The maximum percentages of tumor shrinkage are plotted in a waterfall plot ( A ). The dashed lines indicate RECIST thresholds for tumor reduction or growth. Spider plots of change in tumor size (%) over time were plotted for each dose level ( B ). Only tumors with target lesions were graphed on this spider plot. Dashed lines indicate RECIST thresholds for tumor growth or reduction.
