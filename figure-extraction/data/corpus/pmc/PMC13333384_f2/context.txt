Kaplan–Meier survival curves for the entire cohort.  (A)  Overall survival (OS).  (B)  Intracranial progression-free survival (iPFS). Overall survival and intracranial progression-free survival For the entire cohort, the 1-, 3-, and 5-year OS rates were 83.7%, 34.8%, and 14.1%, respectively, with a median OS of 28.0 months ( Figure 2 ). The 1-, 3-, and 5-year iPFS rates were 54.8%, 10.4%, and 0.7%, respectively, with a median iPFS of 12.4 months ( Figure 2 ).
