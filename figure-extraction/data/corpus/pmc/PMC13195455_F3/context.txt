Spider plot showing the percent change in maximum tumor diameter from baseline in all patients treated with lenvatinib ( n  = 44) Each line represents the best percentage change in the largest measurable tumor for an individual patient. Baseline tumor burden: median tumor size by RECIST, 45.0 mm (range, 26.4–124.0); median diameter of the single largest lesion, 26.1 mm (15.2–124.0). Best overall response: CR 1, PR 22, SD 19, PD 2; ORR 52.3%; DCR 95.5%. Median maximum tumor change: –28.8% (range, –100 to +335.8). Median time to first response: 3 months. The red triangles indicate the time points when treatment was switched from lenvatinib to BRAF/MEK inhibitors, and the blue triangles indicate when treatment was switched from BRAF/MEK inhibitors to lenvatinib. Abbreviations : CR, complete response; PR, partial response; SD, stable disease; PD, progressive disease; ORR, objective response rate; DCR, disease control rate; mos., months; LEN, lenvatinib; BRAF/MEK, BRAF/MEK inhibitor.
