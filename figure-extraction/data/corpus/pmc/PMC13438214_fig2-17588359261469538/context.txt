PFS (a) and OS (b) of the tepotinib and SoC groups after MAIC. CI, confidence interval; HR, hazard ratio; M, months; MAIC, matching-adjusted indirect comparison; OS, overall survival; PFS, progression-free survival; SoC, standard of care. Consistent with before MAIC ( eSupplemental Result 2 ), the tepotinib arm showed significantly higher PFS than the SoC arm (median PFS (95% CI), 16.33 months (9.41–49.54 months) vs 4.77 months (3.19–5.92 months); p < 0.001) after MAIC ( Figure 2(a) ). Based on a Cox proportional hazard model ( eSupplemental Result 3.1 ; eFigures R3-1 and R3-2 ), tepotinib showed a significant PFS benefit over SoC after MAIC (HR (95% CI), 0.38 (0.22–0.63), p < 0.001; Figure 2(a) ). Median OS was not significantly different between the two groups (median OS (95% CI): tepotinib 32.80 months (16.33–NA months); SoC 26.7 months (19.4–NAmonths); log-rank p = 0.90; Figure 2(b) ).
