Tumor response and progression pattern across treatment groups. (a) BOR by treatment group, including SD, PR, PD, and NE, in patients treated with Gem-NabP, mFOLFIRINOX, and NALIRIFOX. (b) Distribution of progression patterns among treatment groups, including progression limited to iPLP and OPP. A significant difference was observed among groups ( p  = 0.115). (c) ORR according to treatment group. ORR was 18.18% in the Gem-NabP group, 30.77% in the NALIRIFOX group, and 16.36% in the mFOLFIRINOX group, with no statistically significant difference among groups ( p  = 0.171). (d) DCR according to treatment group. DCR was 64.46% in the Gem-NabP group, 89.74% in the NALIRIFOX group, and 76.36% in the mFOLFIRINOX group, showing a statistically significant difference among groups ( p  = 0.007). BOR, best overall response; DCR, disease control rate; Gem-NabP, gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel; iPLP, isolated primary-lesion progression; mFOLFIRINOX, modified FOLFIRINOX; NE, not evaluable; OPP, other progression patterns; ORR, objective response rate; PD, progressive disease; PR, partial response; SD, stable disease.
