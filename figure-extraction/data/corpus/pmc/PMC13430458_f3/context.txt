Comparison of first-line cranial radiotherapy status within the serplulimab cohort. Survival curves of  (A)  intracranial progression-free survival (iPFS);  (B)  extracranial PFS;  (C)  systemic PFS; and  (D)  overall survival (OS). This analysis compares patients who received first-line cranial radiotherapy versus those who did not, specifically within the cohort of patients treated with serplulimab and chemotherapy.
